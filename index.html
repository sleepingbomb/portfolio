Kinematic Analysis & Robot Manipulation

RAS 545 · Robotic Systems · Spring 2025

Developed a full robotic manipulation pipeline in MATLAB covering forward & inverse kinematics, Denavit–Hartenberg parameterization, coordinate transformations, trajectory generation, and workspace visualization. Simulated multi-DOF serial manipulators using the Robotics Toolbox — validating kinematic models against analytical solutions and deploying trajectory plans for pick-and-place automation tasks.

CNN Image Classifier for Fashion Recognition

Deep Learning · Computer Vision · 2025–26

Designed, trained, and optimized Convolutional Neural Networks to classify 70,000 grayscale garment images (T-shirts, trousers, sneakers, bags, and more) across 10 categories. Implemented data augmentation, batch normalization, dropout regularization, and hyperparameter search — achieving high accuracy with evaluation via confusion matrices, precision-recall curves, and class-wise analysis.

Laminate-Based Foldable Robot

Foldable Robotics · Bio-Inspired Design · 2025–26

Designed, simulated, and fabricated a laminate-based foldable robotic mechanism using compliant joint principles and origami-inspired folding patterns. Built kinematic and dynamic models in Python (foldable_robotics library), optimized fold geometry through parametric simulation, and validated the prototype through laser-cut fabrication and physical motion testing — targeting deployable robots for confined-space exploration.

Published Research

Peer-reviewed work at the intersection of sustainable energy and environmental engineering.

IEEE Conference Paper

Solar Driven Microwave-Assisted Vacuum Pyrolysis of Plastic Wastes

2021
IEEE Conference
IEEE Xplore Digital Library

Proposed the design and study of a solar power-driven microwave-assisted vacuum pyrolysis system to convert non-biodegradable municipal plastic waste into fuel. The research presents a prototype implementation of an off-grid solar-powered microwave oven for heating the pyrolysis reactor — addressing both the environmental pollution crisis of plastic waste and the need for cost-effective, eco-friendly energy solutions.
